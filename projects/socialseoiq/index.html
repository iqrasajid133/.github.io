Case study

SocialSEO IQ: captions, hashtags and keywords in one place

SocialSEO IQ is a free AI-powered web tool that turns a topic into platform-specific social media content. It is one of my own projects.

Visit SocialSEO IQ ↗All projects

Overview

Writing a good caption, choosing hashtags and thinking about keywords for each platform takes time. SocialSEO IQ brings those jobs together: you describe a topic and choose your tone, industry and goal, and the tool prepares content tailored to each selected platform.

The project combines my work in social media design, web development and AI solutions, and it is a working example of how I approach digital products.

Tagline: “Create Better Social Content. Get More Reach.”

socialseoiq.uk · Designed and built by Iqra Sajid

Features

What it does

Platform-specific output

Content for Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn, tailored to each platform.

Tone, industry and goal

Choose from tones such as professional, casual, inspirational, humorous, educational or luxury, plus an industry and a primary goal such as engagement, sales or brand awareness.

Everything in one result

Captions, detailed descriptions, hashtags, SEO keywords and calls to action.

Extra guidance

A predicted engagement score and suggested posting times.

Easy to use

One-click copy for each section, plus regenerate and clear buttons, with no sign-up required.

My role

What I did

  • Interface and brand design for the tool.
  • Web development and integration of the AI content generation.

The site describes the tool as AI-powered. It has a daily generation limit.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does SocialSEO IQ do?

It generates captions, descriptions, hashtags, SEO keywords and calls to action for Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn from a topic you enter.

Is it free?

It is free to use and does not require sign-up, but there is a reasonable daily generation limit.

Can I edit the results?

Yes. Each section can be copied with one click so you can edit it before publishing.

Who made SocialSEO IQ?
