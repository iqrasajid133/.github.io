Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-15. This page explains what information this website handles and why.

Who I am

This website, iqrasajid.com, is operated by Iqra Sajid (Iqra Sajid Creative Solutions) in Salalah, Oman. You can contact me at iqrasajid.designer@gmail.com.

Information you send me

If you use the contact form, the website collects the name, email address, subject and message you enter. The form submits this information to Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a third-party form service, which delivers it to my email inbox. I use it only to read and reply to your enquiry. If you email or phone me directly, I receive the details you choose to share.

Please do not send sensitive personal information, such as identity documents or payment details, through the form.

Information collected automatically

  • Cookies and storage. This website does not set cookies and does not use browser storage of its own, and it does not run analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Hosting logs. Like most websites, the hosting provider may record technical request data, such as IP address, browser type and requested page, for security and operation.
  • Google Fonts. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches them, Google receives your IP address and standard request information, as described in Google’s own privacy policy.

Third-party websites

This website links to external sites such as Behance, LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X, Google, PenguPix (pengupix.online) and SocialSEO IQ (socialseoiq.uk). Those sites have their own privacy practices, and this policy does not cover them.

How long I keep information

I keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to handle your request and for ordinary business records. You can ask me to delete your messages at any time.

Your choices

You can ask me what information I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, or ask me to stop contacting you, by emailing iqrasajid.designer@gmail.com.

Children

This website is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If the way this website handles information changes, for example if analytics are added, I will update this page and its date.
