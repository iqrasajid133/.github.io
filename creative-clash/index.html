About Creative Clash

A name built on creative energy.

Creative Clash is a creative brand and community introduced by Iqra Sajid Creative Solutions. It is made for people who make things: designers, artists and innovative minds who enjoy testing ideas, stretching their craft and seeing what others create.

The identity says it plainly. Bold, angular lettering, a confident pink on black, and a single line that sets the tone: Create. Compete. Win. It is a brand that celebrates the spark of making something original.

Creative Clash is announced as “a new creative movement”, and its home for updates is the official LinkedIn page.

Our vision

Creative minds, together.

01

Bring creative minds together

Creativity grows faster in company. Creative Clash aims to be a place where people who care about design and art can find one another.

02

Encourage original thinking

The best work starts from a fresh idea rather than a formula. Creative Clash puts original thinking at the centre of everything it stands for.

03

Celebrate creativity

Good ideas deserve an audience. The brand exists to recognise the effort, imagination and craft behind creative work of every kind.

A community for creators

Room for every kind of maker.

Creative Clash is for graphic designers, illustrators, artists, digital creators and anyone who thinks differently. Whether you are just finding your style or have been making work for years, the aim is a space that respects your craft and your curiosity.
