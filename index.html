About

Design with purpose, not decoration.

I’m Iqra Sajid, a graphic designer and digital creative based in Salalah, Oman. For more than six years I’ve helped businesses build brands, publish consistent social content, launch websites and use AI tools to move faster without losing quality.

Every project starts with understanding what the business needs, then turning it into a clear, memorable visual and digital presence.
