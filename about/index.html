About Iqra Sajid

I’m a graphic designer and digital creative based in Salalah, Oman, with more than six years of experience creating brands, websites and digital content.

6+years of
creative work

Creative work with a clear reason.

My work covers the full path from an idea to something people can see and use: a logo, a poster, a social media system, a website or a digital tool. I enjoy projects where design, technology and communication meet.

I work in English and Urdu, and I design in English and Arabic for clients in Oman and beyond. Along the way I have built my own web projects, including PenguPix and SocialSEO IQ, which show how I approach design and development together.

Every engagement begins by understanding what the business needs, who it is speaking to and what action it hopes to inspire. From there, I design something clear, memorable and ready to use.
